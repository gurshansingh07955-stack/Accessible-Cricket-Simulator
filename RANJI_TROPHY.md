# Ranji Trophy 2026-27

Tournament > Domestic tournament. Files: `logic/Ranji.kt` (teams, squads, grounds, fixtures, the knockouts), `logic/FirstClass.kt` (points and tables), `ui/tournament/RanjiTable.kt` (the group tables and promotion and relegation). Own saved tournament: `saved_tournament_ranji_2026_27.json`; own part-match file `saved_ranji_match.json`.

## The competition in the game
- 38 real teams: Elite Groups A to D (eight each) and the Plate group (six). 127 real group matches (28 in each Elite group, 15 in the Plate group), in the real order (round by round, group by group). A match whose ground was not announced is at the first-named side's home ground.
- Squads: the first-round squads from Wisden (35 teams), Haryana's from Sportscafe, Arunachal Pradesh's from the BCCI-appointed panel's announcement (October 2026), and **Sikkim's is last season's**. About 586 players. Players already in the game (the roster and every league) are copied with their ratings; the rest (most of them) are new with **estimated ratings**: from a strength guess for the team (tier 1 to 5, `TEAM_TEXT`), the role and a steady "random" number from the name. The role comes from Wikipedia's infobox where the player has a page (about half), otherwise from the order of the squad list (batters first, bowlers last), with at least one wicketkeeper and five bowlers or all-rounders a team. The Ranji teams are not yet in Advanced Customisation.
- Grounds: about 45, all **estimated** (pitch, boundary size, altitude) in `VENUE_TEXT`.
- League matches are four days (`Ranji.LEAGUE_DAYS`), the knockouts five, with the first-class rules of the five-day Test (`TEST_MATCH.md`): follow-on at a lead of 150 (200 in five days), declarations, the new ball, the pitch wearing, a draw when the overs run out. No DRS, no rain. You play your own matches (Playing XI, toss, then the match, as in the other tournaments) or the computer plays them (`CplSimulator.simulateFirstClass`, the `TEST_*` dials in `AiBallModel.kt`: mid-rated sides about 290 runs in 90 overs at 3 an over, measured on a model of the ball).
- Points (the BCCI's): outright win 6, or 7 by an innings or ten wickets; tie 3 each; in a draw the first-innings leader 3 and the other side 1 (1 each when level or when a first innings was not finished); loss 0. Tables by points, then wins, then the quotient (runs per wicket scored over runs per wicket conceded).
- **Knockouts** (created when the last group match is played): quarter-finals A1 v C2, B1 v D2, C1 v A2, D1 v B2 (the pairing of the other BCCI red-ball and white-ball knockouts; **an assumption**, the BCCI had not published it for this season), semi-finals QF1 v QF4 and QF2 v QF3, the Final. The first-named (group-winning or higher-placed) side is at home; the Final is at the first semi-final winner's home ground (**an assumption**). A drawn or tied knockout goes to the side ahead on the first innings (the first-named side if level). The Plate Final is between the top two of the Plate group (four days).
- **Promotion and relegation**, shown under the tables: the Plate Final winner is promoted; the side that finishes bottom of the four Elite groups together (points, then wins, then quotient) is relegated (one each, as in 2026-27's changes: Bihar up, Nagaland down).
- After the Final: the celebration, Player of the Series and the stats, as in the other tournaments.
