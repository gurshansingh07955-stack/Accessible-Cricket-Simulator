# Career Mode, part twenty-seven (captaincy at every level, a list of competitions on the Points table)

- **State captaincy** (`CareerLeadership.statePromotion`, `CareerProfile.stateLeadership`): a player in the state squad is made vice-captain after five state matches with an average impact of about 30, and captain five state matches later with about 34 (the club's are 28 and 32; both scaled by the career difficulty). The state captain always plays, and in a state tournament he picks the XI, does the toss, chooses the bowlers, sets the field and decides the batting order, as the club, national and franchise captains do (`CareerSeason.captainIn`). He loses it if the state selectors drop him back to district cricket. The averages are the game's own numbers, not measured on play.
- **Career > Captaincy** now lists every captaincy: school, college or club (it stays listed while he plays state or international cricket), the state squad, the nation and a franchise, each with how far he is from the next rank (matches played and the average impact the selectors read).
- **Points table tab** is a list of every competition of the season by its full name (club leagues, state tournaments, international series and tournaments, every franchise league he signed for); each line says the kind, his team, matches played and the champions. Open one for its points table and statistics, with a button back to the list. Before, the competitions were a row of tabs that all said "League" for a franchise league.
- **School and college captains:** the captain path (XI, toss, bowlers, field, batting order) is the same code at school, college and district level (`CareerSeason.captainIn`, `MatchScreen`); no break was found by reading it. The Captaincy page now shows the rank and the progress so a missing promotion is visible.
