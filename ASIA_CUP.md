# Asia Cup 2025 (patch 048)

Tournament > International tournament > Asia Cup 2025 (the men's T20 Asia Cup, UAE, September 2025).

* **8 teams, 2 groups of 4**: Group A India, Pakistan, UAE, Oman; Group B Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Afghanistan,
  Hong Kong. Each plays the other three once (12 group matches, real order, real grounds in Dubai and Abu Dhabi).
* **Super 4**: the top two of each group, a round-robin of six matches (no points carried over), in the real
  order: B1 v B2, A2 v A1, B1 v A2, A1 v B2, A2 v B2, A1 v B1. The top two play the **Final** (Dubai: assumed).
* **Squads**: the 2025 squads (Hong Kong's is the 20-man squad they named). **Hong Kong is a new national team.**
  Players the game already had keep their ratings; 33 are new with ESTIMATED ratings (mostly Hong Kong, Oman,
  UAE); hand and bowling style come from Wikipedia where a page existed.
* T20, 20 overs a side. Points: 2 for a win, 1 for no result; the table orders by points, wins, net run rate.
* Not built: the real results, rain rules.
