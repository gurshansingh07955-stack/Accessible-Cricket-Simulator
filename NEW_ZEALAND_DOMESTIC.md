# New Zealand domestic cricket 2026-27: the Plunket Shield, the Ford Trophy and the Super Smash

Tournament > Domestic tournament now also lists the **Plunket Shield 2026-27** (four-day), the **Ford Trophy 2026-27** (50 overs) and the **Super Smash 2026-27** (T20). Code: `logic/NzDomestic.kt`, `ui/tournament/NzTable.kt`. Own saved tournament each. The six major associations: Auckland Aces, Canterbury Kings, Central Stags, Northern Districts, Otago Volts, Wellington Firebirds (names are the same in all three; Northern Districts are the Northern Brave in the real Super Smash, Canterbury the Kings).

Plunket Shield: 8 rounds, 24 matches, starting 19 November (the first four rounds before the Christmas break, the last four from 1 March), no final. Ford Trophy: 10 rounds and two finals (Elimination Final 19 February, Grand Final 21 February, both at the Basin Reserve). Super Smash: the last season in its current form, 10 rounds from 27 December, Elimination Final and Final at Kennards Hire Community Oval, Auckland on 28 and 30 January.
- **The Ford Trophy has already begun in real life** (28 October); the game starts all three competitions from the beginning and simulates every result. - **Squads**: the 16-man association contract lists for 2026-27 (NZC, 9 July 2026) plus the BLACKCAPS on central contracts, each with his association (for example Latham, Conway, Santner, Phillips, Conway). Players already in the game keep their ratings; the rest are new with **estimated ratings**; several have a guessed role. The final contract each association adds later, and uncontracted players, are not included.

## Rules
- **Plunket Shield points** (NZC): 12 for a win, 6 for a tie, 0 for a draw or a loss; batting bonus 1 point each at 200, 250, 300 and 350 runs; bowling bonus 1 point each for 3, 5, 7 and 9 wickets, from each side's first innings. The real rule counts only the first 110 overs of the innings; the game counts the whole innings. Order: points, wins, then net runs per wicket.
- **Ford Trophy and Super Smash**: 4 points for a win, 2 for a tie or no result; the real Ford Trophy bonus point is not built, and the Super Smash points are an assumption. The top side goes straight to the final, the second and third play an Elimination Final. A tied Ford Trophy league match is shared; a Super Smash tie goes to a Super Over. No DRS.
