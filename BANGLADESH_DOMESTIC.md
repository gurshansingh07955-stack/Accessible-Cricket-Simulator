# Bangladesh domestic cricket 2026-27, part one: the National Cricket League

Tournament > Domestic tournament now also lists the **National Cricket League 2026-27**, the first-class competition of the eight divisions. Code: `logic/BdDomestic.kt`, `ui/tournament/BdTable.kt`. Own saved tournament.

## What is real
- **Fixtures**: the BCB's published list (25 September 2026): seven rounds, 28 matches, each division plays the other seven once, at the published grounds in the published order. The league began on 3 October and has reached round 5 in real life; the game starts from the beginning and simulates every result. **No final**: the top of the table are the champions (the real competition works the same way).
- **Divisions**: Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi, Barishal, Sylhet, Rangpur, Mymensingh. The grounds are shared among the divisions, so the first-named side is not a real home side. The Bir Shrestha Flight Lieutenant Matiur Rahman Stadium (Chattogram) is played at the game's Chattogram ground, a guess.
- **Squads**: the BCB's final 15-man squads of 25 September 2026; the national players are not in them. A player already in the game keeps his ratings; the rest are new with **estimated ratings**, and nearly all have a guessed role (the lists give no roles).
- **Grounds**: Mirpur, Sylhet, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi and Bogura were in the game; **added** (estimated ratings): the Cox's Bazar Academy Ground and the SICS Academy Ground (Sylhet Outer).

## Points (assumed)
The BCB's exact points could not be confirmed. The game uses: 12 for a win, 6 for a tie, 4 for a draw, plus batting bonus (1 point each at 200, 250, 300, 350 runs) and bowling bonus (1 point each for 3, 5, 7, 9 wickets) from the first innings. Order: points, wins, runs per wicket scored over conceded.

## Not built (part two)
Bangladesh's other domestic competitions could not be built from real data: the Dhaka Premier Division one-day league is a club competition whose 2026-27 edition is not scheduled, the National Cricket League T20 has no published fixtures, and the Bangladesh Cricket League (zonal first-class) was played in April. The Bangladesh Premier League is a franchise league, not a domestic tournament.
