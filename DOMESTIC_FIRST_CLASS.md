# Duleep Trophy 2026-27 and Irani Cup 2026-27

Tournament > Domestic tournament now opens a short list: the Ranji Trophy (see `RANJI_TROPHY.md`), the Duleep Trophy and the Irani Cup. Files: `logic/DomesticFc.kt` (squads, grounds, fixtures, the Duleep knockout) and `ui/tournament/DomesticTabs.kt` (the Table page, which has no table for these two). Each has its own saved tournament (`saved_tournament_duleep_2026.json`, `saved_tournament_irani_2026.json`) and part-match file.

## Duleep Trophy
- Six zones: Central, East, North, North East, South, West. Quarter-final 1: East v North East; quarter-final 2: North v West; semi-final 1: Central v the winner of quarter-final 1; semi-final 2: South v the winner of quarter-final 2; the Final. Four days each (the real tournament: BCCI Centre of Excellence, Bengaluru; the Final at Chepauk, Chennai). The first-named side is at home. A drawn match goes to the side ahead on the first innings (the first-named side if level).
- Squads: the 13 or 14 players each zone named (Wikipedia, 2026-27 Duleep Trophy; Sarfaraz Khan, struck out of the West squad, is left out). The Bengaluru grounds are ESTIMATED (balanced pitch).

## Irani Cup
- One five-day match at Sher-i-Kashmir Stadium, Srinagar: Jammu & Kashmir (the Ranji Trophy champions, captain Paras Dogra) v Rest of India (captain Rishabh Pant). The squads are the 15 named for the real match (Sportsdunia, 30 September 2026). A draw goes to the first-innings leader, as in the real competition. The cup is the "Final" of this one-match tournament, so the celebration works as in the others.

## Players
Players already in the game (the roster, the leagues, the Ranji Trophy squads) keep their ratings. The rest are new with ESTIMATED ratings: the team's strength (tier 1 to 5), the role and a steady number made from the name. Roles and bowling styles are from general knowledge of the players, or from the order of the squad list where unknown; a few keepers (Aayush Pandey, Robin Limboo, Ricky Bhui) are guesses.

## Not built
The real results (the game simulates them), the zone squads' reserves, and these teams in Advanced Customisation.
