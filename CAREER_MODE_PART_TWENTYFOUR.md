# Career Mode, part twenty-four (the news and the press conference)

## First part (patch 110): the news after every match
- `logic/CareerNews.kt`. After a match of the player's (played or simulated): the result, what he did (a hundred, a fifty, a five-wicket haul, three or more wickets, 30 or more runs, Player of the Match) and, in a final, the title. After every step of the season: the real results of the other matches just played in his competitions (knockout matches first, at most two) and one or two made-up headlines about world cricket (an international result, a star's performance, a series, a squad, an injury scare). The made-up ones are not real results; `GENERATED_WORLD_NEWS = false` removes them. `NEWS_AFTER_EVERY_MATCH = false` brings back the old way.
- `CareerProfile.newsFeed` (`NewsItem(text, mine)`, the last 80 lines). The season-end lines of `CareerMedia` are put in the feed too; older saves keep their `media` lines, shown first.
- Career tab > In the news: two tabs, All and My news, the newest first.

## Second part (patch 111): the press conference, in text
- `logic/CareerPress.kt` (the questions and the rules) and `ui/career/CareerPressScreen.kt` (the page).
- **When:** after a Player of the Match award (a match he played) or a Player of the Series award (the competition has just ended) in an international match, an international tournament or a franchise league. Not at club or state level. `CareerProfile.pendingPress` keeps it until it is held, so it also comes after a simulated match, or after the app was closed. The newest award replaces an older one that was not held yet. `PRESS_CONFERENCES = false` switches it off.
- **What:** six questions: an opener, the award, what he did (batting, bowling or both), the match (a win or a defeat), and two more from pressure (always asked in a final or knockout), the team, what comes next and his life. 31 questions in all, each with four or five answers in a tone: Humble, Team first, Confident, Bold, Careful. The context picks which variant is asked; the trigger's `seed` fixes the six.
- **What it changes:** `pressMood` (what the papers make of the answers; each answer carries its own mood) and one line in the news. It does not touch ratings or selection. Skipping costs one point of mood.
- **Page:** Skip (first button), the opening words, then for each question the reporter's question, the answers (each with its tone), what he answered, and Next question or Finish.

## Third part (patch 112): the voices
- **Voices (ElevenLabs, web app endpoint `press_tts_generate`, clips at `/_cdn/press/<id>.mp3`):** four reporters who take turns, so each question is asked by a different reporter than the one before (question number n is asked by reporter n mod 4: `r0` Alice, a clear British woman; `r1` Adam, a deep firm man; `r2` Lily, a warm British woman; `r3` Liam, a younger quick man), and ONE voice for the player's answers (Charlie, a relaxed Australian man). All are ElevenLabs premade voices, none heard by the assistant: to change one, change its id in `VOICE_ID_MAP` in the endpoint, republish, and regenerate that voice's clips.
- **Clip ids:** a question is `<question id>_r0` to `_r3` (the same text in four voices), an answer is `<question id>_a` to `_e`. `tools/press_clip_ids.py` works the list out from `CareerPress.kt` (250 clips); `tools/fetch_audio.sh` downloads them into the pack. The question and answer texts never hold a name or a number, so a recording always matches its text.
- **Room sounds (ElevenLabs sound generation, `/_cdn/sfx/`):** `press_camera` (photographers' flashes: at the start and after each answer), `press_hush` (the room goes quiet before each question), `press_murmur` (generated; not used yet), `press_applause` (at the end). Not heard by the assistant.
- **In the app:** `SoundEngine.playPress / playPressRoom / hasPressClip / stopPressVoice` (two `AuctionVoice` queues: the voices, and the room sounds under them). A missing clip, or sound effects off, falls back to the phone's own speech. When a recorded voice says a line, the screen reader does not read it again. Choosing an answer cuts off the question at once.
- **After this patch:** run "Fetch audio into the app", then "Build APK".
