# Career Mode, part twenty-nine (giving up and taking back captaincies)

**Career > Captaincy** now lets the player step down and come back.
- **National captaincy** (or vice-captaincy): "Give up the captain of [country]" (asks first). He stays in the national squad as a player and the selectors do **not** name him again until he asks (`CareerInternational.captaincy` leaves him alone while `CareerProfile.nationalRoleLeft` is set; it stores the role and the country, "Captain|India"). "Take the captain of [country] back" asks the selectors: they agree if he is still in the squad of that country and his leadership score is at least 3.0 (the score that keeps a captain, `leadershipScore`), else they say what is missing. His national contract is lower without the role (`CareerFinance`).
- **Franchise captaincy, league by league:** every franchise he captains this season is listed with its league (IPL, Big Bash ...): "Give up the captaincy of [team] in the [league]" (asks first) removes **only that franchise** from `leagueCaptainTeamIds`; he stays captain in the others. The franchise he gave up (`CareerProfile.leagueCaptainLeft`, this season only) is listed with "Take the captaincy of [team] back". A franchise he never captained is not listed.
- A match of a league he is no longer captain in is played as a player: the franchise's selectors may drop him by his rank, and no XI or toss is his.
- **A new captain takes over.** When he gives a captaincy up, the page names the team's best-rated other player (the higher of batting and bowling rating, plus a tenth of fielding) as the new captain (`CareerSeason.nextCaptainName`). In every career match the XI's captain and vice-captain are now named by `CareerSeason.withLeaders`: he is captain only where he really is one; otherwise the best-rated other player is captain and the next best vice-captain (he himself when he is the vice-captain there). Match info and the scorecards show them. Before, they showed the two highest-rated players, which could be him even when he was not captain.
- **Not built:** the franchise naming someone else as captain while he is away (the captaincy waits for him), the state captaincy (stepping down there; it ends when he leaves the state squad), and the school, college and club captaincies.
