# T20 World Cup 2026 (patch 045)

The game's first international tournament: Tournament > International tournament > T20 World Cup 2026.

* **20 teams, 4 groups of 5** (A: India, Namibia, Netherlands, Pakistan, USA; B: Australia, Ireland, Oman,
  Sri Lanka, Zimbabwe; C: England, Italy, Nepal, Scotland, West Indies; D: Afghanistan, Canada,
  New Zealand, South Africa, UAE). Bangladesh did not play: Scotland replaced them, as in real life.
* **The 40 group matches are the real fixtures in the real order**, at the eight real grounds (Ahmedabad,
  Chennai, Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, Colombo x2, Kandy): all already in the game.
* **Super 8**: the top two of each group, in two groups of four (round-robin, no points carried over).
  The ICC's seeded places are used: Group 1 India, Australia, West Indies, South Africa; Group 2 Pakistan,
  Sri Lanka, England, New Zealand. A qualifying team takes its own seeded place; an unseeded team takes the
  place of a seeded team that did not qualify (as Zimbabwe took Australia's). Match order and grounds of the
  12 Super 8 matches are the real ones.
* **Semi-finals** (Eden Gardens, Wankhede): winner of Super 8 Group 1 v runner-up of Group 2, and winner of
  Group 2 v runner-up of Group 1. **Final** at Narendra Modi Stadium. (If the real tournament had put India
  in a different place, the venues would have changed; here they are fixed.)
* **Squads**: the final 2026 squads (15 to 18 players, replacements included). Players the game already
  had keep their ratings; 89 are new with ESTIMATED ratings (all of Namibia, Italy and Canada, and the
  players missing from the other associate squads). Only the Table tab shows a table per group.
* No overseas / breakout rules: any XI of 11 is allowed. Tags are all plain squad members.
* Points: 2 for a win, 1 for no result, ties settled by a Super Over; the table orders by points, wins, then
  net run rate (the ICC's later tie-breakers are not applied).
* Not built: the real results are not used (the game simulates them), rain rules, the Women's World Cup.
