# Pakistan domestic cricket 2026-27, part one: the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold and Silver

Tournament > Domestic tournament now also lists the **Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold 2026-27** and the **Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Silver 2026-27**, both first-class (four days, five in the Final). Code: `logic/PakDomestic.kt`, `ui/tournament/PakTable.kt`. Own saved tournament each. **Part two (not built):** the National T20 Cup (Silver 5 to 19 February 2027, Gold 23 February to 6 March; the fixtures are not published yet), the President's Trophy and the one-day cups.

## What is real
- **Structure**: the PCB split the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy into two divisions for 2026-27 and let Peshawar and Sialkot field two teams each. Gold: Karachi Blues (defending champions), Faisalabad, FATA, Lahore Whites, Islamabad, Peshawar Blues, Multan, Sialkot Blues: 28 league matches and a five-day Final (15 to 19 December). Silver: twelve teams in two groups of six, 30 matches, and a Final between the group winners (3 to 7 December); the winner is promoted to Gold next season (not built).
- **Fixtures and grounds**: the published lists (PCB, 2 November 2026), in the real order. The first round was played on 3 to 6 November in real life; the game starts both from the beginning and simulates every result. The Finals' grounds are not announced: the game plays the Gold Final at the National Cricket Ground, Islamabad, and the Silver Final at Multan.
- **Squads**: the PCB rosters of 2 November 2026 (the Gold squads also include the reserves and the national-duty stars such as Shan Masood, Abrar Ahmed and Mir Hamza). A player already in the game keeps his ratings; the rest are new with **estimated ratings**, and most have a guessed role (the lists give no roles, only the wicketkeepers).
- **Grounds**: Multan, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad (Iqbal Stadium), Gaddafi, the National Stadium Karachi, Niaz Stadium Hyderabad and Sheikhupura were in the game; **added** (estimated ratings): National Cricket Ground and Diamond Cricket Ground (Islamabad), Abbottabad, Shoaib Akhtar Stadium (Rawalpindi), SBP and UBL Sports Complexes and KCCA Stadium (Karachi), Sports Complex Rahim Yar Khan and Dring Stadium (Bahawalpur).

## Points (assumed)
The PCB's exact points could not be confirmed. The game uses: 16 for a win, 8 for a tie, 5 for a draw, plus batting bonus (1 point at 200 runs, then 1 for each 50 more, up to 5) and bowling bonus (1 for 3 wickets, 2 for 6, 3 for 9) from the first innings. Order: points, wins, runs per wicket scored over conceded. A drawn Final goes to the side that led on the first innings.

## Not built
The National T20 Cup, the President's Trophy, the National Champions Cup and the departmental one-day cup, promotion to Gold, and the women's competitions.
