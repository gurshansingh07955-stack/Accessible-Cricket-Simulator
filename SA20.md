# SA20 2027 (tournament)

Tournament > League tournaments > SA20 2027 (Betway SA20 Season 5, 17 January to 21 February 2027). Same pages, saving and tabs as the CPL.

**Real facts used** (published July to October 2026): six teams (Durban's Super Giants, Joburg Super Kings, MI Cape Town, Paarl Royals,
Pretoria Capitals, Sunrisers Eastern Cape), the squads they ended the 7 October 2026 auction with (18 or 19 players, wildcards included;
Joburg's wildcard was not yet named), and the fixture format: 30 league matches (every team hosts every other once, at its own ground), then
Qualifier 1 at Boland Park (Paarl), the Eliminator and Qualifier 2 at the Wanderers, and the Final at Newlands: 34 matches, T20.

**Fixture order.** Who hosts whom is certain. The order of the first 16 matches follows the published list. For matches 17 to 30 the sources
disagreed on team names, so the order is reconstructed from dates and grounds: pairings and venues are right, an odd date may be swapped.

**Rules used:** at most four overseas players in the XI. Not built: the league's bonus point (4 for a win, 1 more for a big run-rate win;
the game's table gives 2 a win) and the under-23 rule.

**Players.** Anyone already in the game (roster or an earlier tournament) is copied with his ratings (68 of 113). The rest are new with
**estimated ratings** (the `nw(...)` lines in `logic/Sa20.kt`). South Africans are tagged `DOMESTIC`, everybody else `OVERSEAS`
(Andries Gous plays for the USA, so he counts as overseas). `al(...)` links Ottneil Baartman = Ottniel Baartman and Lungisani Ngidi = Lungi Ngidi.
