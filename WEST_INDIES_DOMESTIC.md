# West Indies domestic cricket, 2027 edition: the West Indies Championship and the Super50 Cup

Tournament > Domestic tournament now also lists the **West Indies Championship 2027** (four-day) and the **Super50 Cup 2026-27 (West Indies)** (50 overs). Code: `logic/WiDomestic.kt`, `ui/tournament/WiTable.kt`. Own saved tournament each.

## What is real and what is not
- **There are no published fixtures for the next editions** (Cricket West Indies has cut its calendar for money reasons). The game makes the schedules in the published formats and says so on the start page. The last real editions were the 2026 West Indies Championship (12 April to 20 May 2026, won by Trinidad and Tobago) and the 2025-26 Super50 Cup (19 to 29 November 2025, won by Barbados Pride).
- **West Indies Championship (2026 format)**: three simultaneous three-match series between the 2026 seeding pairs, Windward v Guyana (Antigua), Jamaica v Barbados (Jamaica) and Leeward v Trinidad and Tobago (Antigua). The series winners are ranked by points; the top one goes straight to the Final, the other two play a Playoff, and its winner meets the top one in the Final (four days each, at the Sir Vivian Richards Stadium). The real top side also played the West Indies Academy as match practice before the Final: not built. The pairs are an assumption for 2027 (they are the 2026 ones).
- **Super50 Cup (2025-26 format)**: six teams, each plays the other five once (15 matches, all in Trinidad, in three grounds in turn), then a Final between the top two at Queen's Park Oval. 4 points a win and 2 for a tie or no result; the real bonus-point system is not built. A tied league match is shared.
- **Squads**: the 2026 championship squads (13 or 14 players each; Wikipedia). Players already in the game (the West Indies roster and the CPL) keep their ratings; the others are new with **estimated ratings**, several with a guessed role. The West Indies Academy is not included.
- **Ground**: Sabina Park, Queen's Park Oval, Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, Providence, Kensington Oval, Arnos Vale and Brian Lara Stadium were in the game; **added** (estimated ratings): the Brian Lara Cricket Academy in Couva.

## Championship points (CWI)
16 for a win, 6 for a tie, 3 each for a draw, plus a batting bonus (1 point for each 50 runs from 250, up to 5) and a bowling bonus (1 point for 3 wickets, 2 for 5, 3 for 7) from the first innings. The real rule counts only the first 110 overs of an innings and adds 0.2 points for each wicket taken by a designated pace bowler: not built.

## Not built
The West Indies Academy match, the Super50 bonus points, women's competitions, the T20 Blaze (it is a women's competition only).
