# Asian Games 2026 (patch 049)

Tournament > International tournament > Asian Games 2026 (the men's cricket event, T20, Kōrogi Sports Park,
Nisshin, Japan, 24 September to 3 October 2026).

* **10 teams.** Six are drawn into two groups of three (Group A Nepal, Afghanistan, Japan; Group B Malaysia,
  Hong Kong, Oman), each team plays the other two once, and the top two of each group go on. Four seeded teams
  wait in the quarter-finals: **India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh**.
* **Quarter-finals (the real draw)**: Pakistan v the Group B runner-up, India v the Group A runner-up, Bangladesh
  v the Group B winner, Sri Lanka v the Group A winner. **Semi-finals**: winner of QF1 v winner of QF3, winner of
  QF2 v winner of QF4. Then the **bronze medal match** and the **gold medal match** (named "Final" so its winner
  is the champion).
* Every match is at **Kōrogi Sports Park**, a ground added to the game (ratings estimated; Nagoya in autumn is
  humid and rain-prone).
* **Squads**: the 15-man squads. Players the game already had keep their ratings; 66 are new with ESTIMATED
  ratings (all of Japan and Malaysia, and the players missing from the other squads: guessed by a tier for the
  team plus role/style from Wikipedia where a page existed). Japan and Malaysia are new national teams. The
  Oman squad is as listed on Wikipedia.
* Points: 2 for a win, 1 for no result; the group tables order by points, wins, net run rate.
* Not built: the real results (in real life the quarter-finals were rained off), rain rules.
