# My Player, part two: the playing style and an optional photo

After the district club the career asks, one page each: role (Batsman, Wicketkeeper-batsman, Bowler, Batting all-rounder, Bowling
all-rounder), batting hand, then, only for a role that bowls, bowling hand and bowling style (Fast, Medium-fast, Medium, and the spin of
the arm: Off break and Leg break for a right-arm bowler, Orthodox spin and Wrist spin for a left-arm bowler), then the batting position
(Top order 1 to 3, Middle order 4 to 6, Low order 7 to 9, Lower order 10 and 11), then, for a bowling role, the bowling phase (Starting
overs, Middle overs, Death overs). The career is saved after the last of them.

A Batsman and a Wicketkeeper-batsman are not asked about bowling (they do not bowl); a Bowler is asked about everything.

The name page has an OPTIONAL profile photo: Choose a profile photo (the phone's gallery or files), change it, remove it, or skip it.
It is shrunk to 384 pixels and saved as files/career_photos/<id>.png; it shows on the career's page and is deleted with the career.
A career saved by part one has no role or photo yet and says so.
