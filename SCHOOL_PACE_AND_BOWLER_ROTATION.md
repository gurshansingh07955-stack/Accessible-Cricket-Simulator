# School bowling speed and the computer's bowling changes (patch 130)

**School pace limit (new careers only).** `CareerProfile.schoolPaceCap` is set to true when a career is created (an older career has no value and keeps the old speeds). While such a career is in its **School** years, `MatchScreen(maxPaceKmh = 135)` sets `BowlingSystem.paceCapKmh`, and every pace ball, the user's own (the speed slider) and the computer's, stays between the stock minimum and **135 km/h** (the stock range 110 to 159 becomes 110 to 135, a cutter 115 to 135; the slower balls and every spin ball are below it already). College and later are as before. The cap is `135` in `CareerHubScreens.kt` (one number). Read from the message "over 140... keep it at 110 to 135"; tell me if you meant other numbers.

**The computer's bowling changes** (`MatchStateMachine.getEligibleBowlers`). Only bowlers and all-rounders could bowl, each for at most a fifth of the innings (4 overs in a T20), so a side with two or three of them ran out after eight or twelve overs and the computer kept the last bowler on (back to back, and beyond his overs). Now a side with fewer real bowlers than the overs need (5 in a T20, 10 overs each in an ODI means 5, and so on) adds its best bowling ratings among the other players as part-timers from the start, so the overs are shared; a bowler never bowls two overs running while anyone else is under the cap (a wicketkeeper last of all). This also applies to the user's own choice list in a match like that. Computer against computer matches use the same rule.
