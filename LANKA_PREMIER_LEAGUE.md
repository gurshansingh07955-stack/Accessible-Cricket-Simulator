# Lanka Premier League 2026 (tournament)

Tournament > League tournaments > Lanka Premier League 2026. Same pages, saving and tabs as the CPL.

**Real facts used** (LPL 6, 17 July to 8 August 2026): five teams (Colombo Kaps, Dambulla Sixers, Galle Gallants, Jaffna Kings,
Kandy Royals), a double round-robin (20 league matches), then Qualifier 1, the Eliminator, Qualifier 2 and the Final: 24 matches.
Grounds as played: the first 5 league matches at the SSC Ground, the next 9 at Rangiri Dambulla, the last 6 at Pallekele, the
play-offs at the R. Premadasa Stadium (all four were already in the game). The franchises have no home ground.

**Squads** are the 2026 draft squads with every announced replacement applied. Players who left on 1 August with no
replacement are out (Moeen Ali, Sediqullah Atal, Brandon McMullen).

**Rules used:** at most four overseas players in the XI; at least one under-23 player in the XI (the game's "breakout" tag marks
the ten Emerging-category under-23 players). **Not built:** the LPL's own Impact Player substitute.

**Players.** Anyone already in the game (roster, CPL or Abu Dhabi T10) is copied with his ratings. About 45 were not in the game
and are new; **their ratings are estimates** (the `nw(...)` lines in `logic/LankaPremierLeague.kt`; the lesser-known young Sri
Lankan players have guessed roles). `al(...)` links Khawaja Nafay = Khwaja Nafay and Shaheen Shah Afridi = Shaheen Afridi.
