# Custom stadiums

Home > Advanced Customisation > **Create Custom Stadiums** (`ui/custom/CustomStadiumsScreens.kt`, `logic/CustomStadium.kt`).

* **The flow**: the name (keyboard, up to 40 characters, unique among the player's stadiums), the type of pitch (Batting, Balanced, Green, Dusty, Bowling), the weather (seven choices), the boundary size (Small, Medium, Large), then a summary with the **average T20 first-innings score** and Save. On the pitch, weather and boundary pages "Copy from a stadium in the game" takes just that one thing from a game ground (a country list, then its grounds).
* **The average score** (`CustomStadiums.score`): a base of 162 (a balanced pitch, medium boundaries, ordinary weather; the game's own grounds average about 161), plus the pitch (batting +14, balanced 0, green -10, dusty -9, bowling -22), plus the boundary (small +6, medium 0, large -7), plus the weather (rain and humidity lower it, heat raises it a little, wind lowers it; at most 10 either way), rounded to the nearest 5. Set against the game's own 113 grounds; the summary says how each part counted. If scores on custom grounds feel off, those constants are the dials.
* **Where they show**: every custom stadium is listed under the country **Custom**. `StadiumData.getAllStadiums`, `getAllFilterCountries`, `getStadiumsForCountry` and `getStadiumById` include them, so the quick-match stadium list, the series host-country list and the custom-tournament stadium picker (all built from those) show a "Custom" country with them, and `CplData.stadium` resolves them for tournaments.
* **Saved** in `custom_stadiums.json`, read when the app starts (`Customisation.init`). A saved tournament, series or match that names a stadium the player has since deleted is played at a plain neutral ground (`CustomStadiums.DELETED`) instead of breaking.
* **Not used**: careers (their fixtures use only the game's own grounds). Altitude is fixed at 100 m. A saved stadium cannot be edited yet: delete it and create it again.
