# Career captain, projected score and knockout labels (patch 157)

* **A career captain is not asked who the captain is** (`PlayingXIScreen(fixedCaptainId)`, passed by the career PickXI page). After he picks the XI he is the captain already: only the vice-captain and the wicketkeeper are asked. The vice-captain he names is now kept (`CareerSeason.withLeaders` used to replace it with the best-rated player). An untouched auto-pick keeps him as captain when he is in the XI. If he is not in the XI the old captain step and the "your own name must be in your XI" check still apply.
* **Projected score** (`WinProbability.projectedScore`, `MatchLines.projectedScoreLine`, on the match screen right under the win probability): in the first innings of a limited-overs match, the score the innings is heading for. The balls left are worth the run rate so far blended with the par rate (the more of the innings has gone, the more the run rate counts, at most 85 percent), scaled by the wickets in hand against what a normal innings would have lost by now. At the start it is the par score of the format and the ground. Not shown in a chase or a Test. `WinProbability.PROJECTED_SCORE` switches it off.
* **Career knockout labels** (`CareerSeason.stageText`, `matchSuffix`): the next-match card says "T20 World Cup 2026, match 41, semi-final: ..." (also "quarter-final", "final", "Group A", "Super 8"; a league match says only "match 12") and adds "This is a knockout match: lose and you are out." for a quarter-final or semi-final, "This is the final." for the final. The same text is the tournament line on the match screen's Match info page and on the result and scorecard text.
