# The umpire's decisions, pad hits and reviews by the bowling side (patch 142)

**Before:** every LBW appeal was given out, and only a ball the game had already decided was LBW could be appealed.

**Now**
- **Appeals on any pad or body hit.** `BallOutcome.padHit` is set by the engine for an LBW the game decided, for a ball that ran off the body for leg byes, and now and then (`UmpireDecisions.PAD_HIT_DOT_CHANCE`, 5%) for a dot ball on the stumps' line. When the user is bowling he is asked to appeal on every one (the screen says only that the ball hit the batter on the pad, never whether it would have hit the stumps). When the user is batting the computer side appeals for an LBW the game decided and for 35% of other pad hits. Not on a wide or a no ball. In career mode, only when the career player is the batter or the bowler.
- **The umpire decides: out or not out** (`UmpireDecisions.umpireGivesOut`). An LBW the game decided is given out 93% of the time for a Perfect ball down to 62% for a Very Bad ball, and otherwise NOT OUT. A pad hit that was not LBW is given not out, except for 6% of the time when the umpire is wrong and gives it OUT (the ball is replayed as an LBW wicket, `MatchSimulation.forceLbw`). A catch, a stumping, a run out and a bowled are never in doubt. `UmpireDecisions.UMPIRE_CAN_GIVE_NOT_OUT = false` brings back the old behaviour for an LBW.
- **Caught behind and stumped too.** A catch behind the stumps (a catch taken by the keeper: `UmpireDecisions.isCaughtBehind`) is given out 88% of the time, a stumping 90%, and otherwise not out; both can be appealed (the appeal screen says "Appeal for caught behind") and, when given not out, reviewed by the fielding side; when given out, a catch behind can now be reviewed by the batter too (UltraEdge: `DrsSystem.buildCaughtBehind`, 8% to 28% overturned, no umpire's call) as a stumping already could. Other catches, bowled and run outs are given as they happened.
- **A NOT OUT can be reviewed by the fielding side** (`FieldingReviewScreen`). It has its own reviews, the same number as the batting side (`MatchState.drsFieldingReviewsUsed`, reset each innings; custom tournaments' DRS setting applies). The user (bowling) is offered "Review" or "Accept the decision" for ten seconds; the computer decides by chance. The third umpire's checks are spoken and the verdict follows: **overturned** to OUT (the review is kept; only possible if the ball really was LBW), **umpire's call** (not out stands, kept), or **not out stands** (review lost). The verdict is rolled in `UmpireDecisions.fieldingReviewVerdict`: a better ball is more likely overturned (85% for Perfect to 45% for Very Bad when it really was LBW); a ball that was not LBW is never overturned.
- **The computer's keenness to review** (both sides) now depends on **the batter's score, his rating and the situation of the match**, not only on timing. Fielding side (`aiFieldingReviewChance`): 55% if the ball really was out, 10% if not, plus up to 25% for the batter's score (100 runs or more), up to 15% for his rating (50 to 100), up to 20% for the pressure of the match (`situationPressure`: a hard chase, and wickets down), at most 92%, and times 0.7 on its last review. Batting side (`aiBatterWantsReview`): the old chance from the timing of his shot, plus up to 20% for his score, 10% for his rating and 15% for the pressure, less 10%.
- **The last-ball summary says what the umpire decided:** "Appeal turned down: not out.", "Not out. The appeal was turned down.", "Review: overturned, given out.", "Review: umpire's call, so not out stands.", "Review: not out stands, and the review is lost." (`ballNote`, added to the summary in `commitBall`). A wicket given out already says so.
- **The batter's review** of an OUT decision is the existing DRS (DrsScreens.kt), including for an out the umpire gave wrongly.

Voices: the umpire says "Out!" or "Not out" in his recorded voice (um_out, um_not_out and their variants). The fielding review speaks in the recorded umpire voices too: the on-field umpire's referral (`um_review_field`), the third umpire's checks (the same clips the batting review uses) and the verdict (`tu_f_overturned`, `tu_f_call`, `tu_f_stands`), plus `tu_cb_carry` and `tu_edge_unclear` for a catch behind. These six clips are new: generate them with the web app's `umpire_tts_generate`, then run "Fetch audio into the app" and "Build APK". Until then, or if one is missing, the phone's voice reads the line. The DRS tone, heartbeat and the firecracker, lose and umpire's-call sounds are the batting review's.

Not built: reviews on run outs for the fielding side; the review count of the fielding side on Match info; an appeal for a faint edge that was not a wicket.
